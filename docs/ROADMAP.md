## Risk Mitigation

### Identified Risks
1. **Integration complexity** → Phased integration with thorough testing
2. **Performance issues** → Load testing and optimization throughout
3. **User adoption** → Intuitive UI/UX and comprehensive training
4. **Data security** → Security-first design and regular audits
5. **API rate limits** → Caching, quota management, and fallbacks

### Contingency Planning
- **30% budget buffer** allocated for unexpected issues
- **Flexible timeline** (18-22 weeks) to accommodate challenges
- **Regular checkpoints** for go/no-go decisions
- **Iterative deployment** strategy to minimize risk
